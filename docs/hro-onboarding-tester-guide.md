# Authorized operation and evidence review

Historical test records are restricted, read-only and permanently non-operational. They cannot dispatch, gain authority, satisfy readiness gates or enter real outcome metrics. Static mappings are not executed assertions, source authentication, vendor certification or evidence of live readiness.

## Navigation and operational queues
Use the Control Tower for authorized operational work. Historical test records are separate, read-only and non-operational.

- Select your authorized workspace.
- Review current operational queues and ownership.
- Use Cases history for retained records, never as evidence of live work.

Evidence:
- Current workspace and scoped access
- Dated operational events

Limits:
- Shared demo identities cannot create cases or acquire real workflow authority.

## Intake and onboarding gates
Ordinary authenticated intake uses real case facts and explicit governance grants.

- Create a case only with current case-flow and profile-edit access.
- Confirm offer acceptance through its dedicated evidence-backed gate.
- Resolve mandatory workstreams before requesting final human readiness review.

Evidence:
- Approved policy snapshot
- Source evidence and approval version
- Current scoped human decision

Limits:
- General intake does not collect free-form candidate notes or medical details.
- A score or status label never substitutes for authority or safety gates.

## Candidate documents and Employee Vault
Private uploads, scanning, authenticity checks and consent are distinct controls.

- Issue candidate access only through authorized ordinary workflows.
- Keep quarantined or uncertain evidence blocked.
- Apply all-required evidence and fresh consent checks to Vault reuse.

Evidence:
- Private file references
- Current scanner and source-verification receipts
- Consent and reuse scope

Limits:
- Local file checks do not prove identity or source authenticity.
- Retained test portals cannot accept new uploads.
- Privacy withdrawal remains independent of new collection approval.

## Human decisions and specialist handoffs
Membership, assignment and mailbox personas are not professional authority.

- Review the exact case and evidence revision.
- Check current specialist role, jurisdiction and scope.
- Record the accountable decision without inventing external completion.

Evidence:
- Scoped authority
- Versioned approval
- Protected audit receipt

Limits:
- Stale decisions and historical test records cannot authorize operations.

## Workforce context, frontline release and early tenure
Approved country-specific policies govern context and safety requirements.

- Confirm work country and approved policy revision.
- Keep medical, safety and BGV gates independent of weighted readiness.
- Record actual start and dated human follow-ups under current authority.

Evidence:
- Policy adoption
- Independent release evidence
- Actual-start revision

Limits:
- Unapproved starter policies are proposals, not runtime authority.
- Do not infer historical success from current checklists.

## Workforce transfers
Transfers require country-specific approval, source-attested execution and evidence-fenced cutover.

- Review population, legal basis and required objections.
- Reconcile source and target evidence.
- Obtain current independent cutover sign-offs.

Evidence:
- Approved country policy
- Source-attested completion
- Generation-bound sign-off

Limits:
- A Go label is not payroll or legal certification.

## AI-supported work and guardrails
Generated plans are advisory; explanations describe recorded evidence rather than hidden reasoning.

- Review drafts before adoption.
- Verify citations and limitations.
- Escalate uncertain or adverse evidence to accountable humans.

Evidence:
- Approved source context
- Recorded limitations
- Reviewed action proposal

Limits:
- AI output never grants authority or proves an external effect.

## Policy library and grounded answers
Source extracts, case evidence and policy decisions remain separate.

- Maintain drafts under scoped access.
- Obtain required review before publication.
- Check audience, jurisdiction, dates and source context at disclosure.

Evidence:
- Versioned source
- Approved extract
- Current publication review

Limits:
- Retired evaluations cannot provide a current accuracy or fairness claim.

## Provider systems and authorized channel checks
Separately authorized operational tests remain available; configuration alone does not prove provider execution.

- Review connector mode and approved estate.
- Use explicit, authorized operational channel checks only.
- Reconcile uncertain effects without automatic non-idempotent redispatch.

Evidence:
- Verified provider receipt
- Stable effect-journal payload
- Current recipient authorization

Limits:
- Historical test records remain permanently denied dispatch.
- No simulated integration is represented as genuine external work.

## Communications and monitored replies
Verified transport, tenant attribution, recipient authorization and responder authority are independent.

- Check the exact case-bound reply reference.
- Quarantine ambiguous attribution.
- Review durable delivery status and unknown outcomes.

Evidence:
- Provider message id
- Verified transport evidence
- Case-bound correlation

Limits:
- A shared mailbox identity alone cannot authorize a state change.

## Operational outcomes and commercial assumptions
Live metrics exclude historical test records. Commercial what-if estimates remain available with stated assumptions.

- Check cohort, dates, source provenance and denominator.
- Distinguish observed outcomes from estimates.
- Do not treat aggregate what-if means as causal forecasts or case-level SLA rates.

Evidence:
- Dated source evidence
- Eligible real cohort
- Explicit modeling assumptions

Limits:
- Historical test activity is not business performance or readiness.

## Governance, privacy, retention and audit
Sensitive access, legal holds, retention, independent audit readiness and release checks remain mandatory.

- Use scoped access reports and current authority.
- Review approved lifecycle policies and legal holds.
- Verify independent audit receipts before regulated release.

Evidence:
- Approved retention policy
- Legal hold
- Independent provider-verified audit receipt

Limits:
- Tenant administration is not specialist authority.
- Restricted history and protected effect journals are retained; retirement does not purge records.
