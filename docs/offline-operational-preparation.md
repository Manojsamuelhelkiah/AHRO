# Offline operational preparation

## Current capability

Authorized named members can save a separate, revisioned real request draft
for an exact, server-defined curated fictional example in the Demo workspace.
The case detail form uses the current family intake template. It supports
incomplete requests and evidence references, but references remain unverified.

Original case identity, provenance, history, checkpoints, approvals and sample
evidence are not changed or adopted by saving. Preparation sends no messages,
issues no contact links and starts no worker or provider transaction.

Shared Demo accounts may view the preparation journey but cannot save real
request information. Viewing a saved draft requires current case, profile,
notes and all applicable sensitive-field read permissions. Saving additionally
requires corresponding edits, named membership and approved collection policies.

The recorded draft country establishes its privacy jurisdiction when the
fictional source has none. A draft does not replace conflicting retained
jurisdiction or its retention obligations. Existing hold-aware envelope
minimisation clears the draft along with other case-derived personal content.
Changing the country of an existing draft requires governed correction and is
not supported by this form.

## Not yet implemented

This is not a complete operational activation feature:

- Explicit revision-bound activation and fresh identity/policy confirmation.
- A current operational identity projection separate from original source data.
- Admission at engine, worker, approval, document, candidate and effect boundaries.
- Fresh runtime generations that exclude historical approvals and sample evidence.
- Supported family-specific continuation and closure.
- Live configured email and external-system operations.
- Dashboard, Analytics and JSON/CSV cohort alignment.
- End-to-end activation, external-effect isolation and responsive-browser validation.

The existing live-effect restrictions intentionally remain in place. A saved
draft must not be interpreted as an activation receipt, authority, integration
readiness or measured business outcome. Real volunteer/controller histories
and actual UAT-run-owned cases remain ineligible. Offboarding closure remains
blocked until section 4.6 is supplied and approved.

## Read-only synthetic journey presentation

Existing verified fictional examples now have a separate fixed journey
illustration, with completed, rejected and blocked branches. This does not
implement any of the operational capabilities listed above. See
[synthetic-journey-verification.md](synthetic-journey-verification.md) for the
eligibility inventory, outcome matrix, served-build diagnosis and verification.
Stored operational state, original source narratives/dates, readiness,
approvals, communications and audit history remain unchanged. Fictional roles
are not assignments; illustrative decisions and source summaries are not
evidence, consent, delivery or release authority.
