## Canonical hierarchy
- **Run and pay workforce**
  - Pay access and personal payroll inputs → **View/download an authenticated pay statement; explain a pay-statement line; update payment method/bank details; update tax-withholding election; submit a voluntary-deduction election; report a missing/incorrect pay statement.**
  - Recurring/on-demand pay → **Submit a recurring-pay change for effective dating; submit an off-cycle/one-time payment request; calculate gross-to-net pay for a pay group; validate payroll input completeness; resolve a payroll calculation exception; approve a payroll result; release a payroll payment; confirm payment received/returned.**
  - Pay correction and reconciliation → **Submit an underpayment/overpayment correction case; correct employee pay or deduction; correct a statutory/tax submission; correct an authority payment; reconcile payroll register to funding/GL/bank result; close a payroll cycle with reconciled evidence.**
  - Statutory reporting/year-end → **Validate jurisdictional payroll reporting data; submit a periodic payroll return; produce/deliver year-end employee tax document; amend a year-end tax document; reconcile payroll liabilities/remittances; retain payroll audit evidence.**
  - Garnishment and levies → **Register a valid garnishment/levy order; validate priority and disposable-income calculation; start/modify/stop a withholding; remit withholding to the authority; resolve a garnishment exception; provide withholding evidence.**
- **Administer compensation, equity, recognition, pensions and financial wellbeing**
  - Base/variable compensation → **Request a salary/rate change; validate a compensation change against policy/range/budget; approve compensation change; apply an effective-dated compensation change; submit bonus/commission input; correct bonus/commission result.**
  - Equity and recognition → **Grant/modify/cancel an equity award instruction; acknowledge vesting/exercise/tax event; nominate recognition; approve recognition award; issue recognition award; correct recognition award.**
  - Retirement/pension and wellbeing → **Enroll/change/cease a pension contribution election; validate pension eligibility/contribution limits; transmit/reconcile pension contribution; resolve pension-provider exception; enroll in financial-wellbeing program; request financial-wellbeing advance/repayment change.**
- **Administer benefits and expenses**
  - Eligibility and enrollment → **Determine benefit eligibility; make/open-enrollment election; submit qualifying-life-event benefit change; validate dependent/life-event evidence; approve/deny exception enrollment; waive/reinstate coverage; calculate benefit deductions.**
  - Carrier and claims → **Transmit enrollment/change to carrier; reconcile carrier eligibility/billing discrepancy; resolve carrier coverage exception; submit reimbursement-benefit claim; approve/deny reimbursement claim; pay/recover reimbursement.**
  - HR-owned expenses → **Submit HR-owned expense/reimbursement claim with receipts; validate policy/tax/eligibility; approve or return expense; reimburse approved expense; correct/recover expense payment.**
- **Capture time, schedule labor and manage attendance**
  - Time capture and corrections → **Create/submit time entry; clock in/out or submit attendance; amend submitted time; submit missing-time correction; validate time against schedule/labor codes; resolve missing/duplicate/invalid time exception; approve time; lock approved time for payroll.**
  - Scheduling and overtime → **Create/change base schedule; publish schedule; request schedule change/swap; approve schedule change; request overtime/comp time/credit hours; approve/cancel overtime; calculate overtime premium/entitlement; provide attendance/time report.**
- **Administer absence and leave**
  - Routine absence → **View absence balance/accrual; request/edit/cancel absence; validate balance and policy; approve/deny absence; correct absence record/accrual; convert approved absence to payable time.**
  - Statutory/protected/extended leave → **Open leave case; determine leave eligibility; collect/validate certification and evidence; approve/deny/designate leave; coordinate intermittent leave and return-to-work; calculate leave pay/top-up; restore/correct leave entitlement; close leave case with evidence.**
- **Control, assure and explain pay-related operations**
  - Compliance and audit → **Answer policy/status inquiry without changing record; validate minimum-wage/overtime/pay-equity rule; investigate privacy/security or segregation-of-duties exception; produce employee/regulator/audit evidence; approve configuration/rate/rule change; deploy and attest payroll/benefit/time rule configuration.**

## Agent and execution mapping
- **Employee inquiries, statements, balances, policy/status (all view leaves):** accountable Payroll/Benefits/Time Service Desk specialist by domain; collaborators HRIS support and local HR. Systems: HCM, payroll, time/absence, benefits portal, knowledge base. Read-only retrieval with identity/entitlement check; no approval except sensitive-data access. Evidence: case transcript, source record ID/version, delivery/access audit.
- **Employee elections and submissions (tax/bank/deduction, benefits/pension, time, absence, expense, compensation request):** accountable domain operations specialist; collaborators employee, manager, HRBP, payroll, benefits vendor. Systems: employee self-service HCM, document store, workflow. Write a pending effective-dated transaction and attach evidence; validate identity, dates, eligibility and duplicates. Employee attestation plus manager approval where policy requires; elevated risk for bank, tax, dependent and pay changes. Evidence: immutable submission, submitted documents, validation outcome, approval trail.
- **Time/schedule/absence leaves:** accountable Time & Attendance or Leave specialist; collaborators manager/timekeeper, payroll, occupational health/legal for protected leave. Systems: scheduling/time clock, absence/leave engine, payroll interface. Create/modify/approve time/schedule/absence records, then send approved payable time. Human approval for timesheets, overtime, exceptions and statutory/protected leave; risks include wage-hour breach, leave privacy and payroll cutoff. Evidence: timestamped punches/entries, approval, balance/entitlement calculation, interface run and exception-resolution log. GSA explicitly separates employee, supervisor, timekeeper and time-administrator roles.
- **Payroll run, payment, corrections, statutory reporting, garnishments:** accountable Payroll Operations specialist; collaborators Treasury/Finance, Tax/Legal, HRIS, payroll provider, authority. Systems: payroll engine, tax service, bank/payment platform, GL, case/workflow. Read inputs and exceptions; write controlled pay results, correction records, submissions and remittance files. Require maker-checker approval for final pay, off-cycle/high-value correction, bank release, legal order and authority filing; risks are monetary loss, tax penalties, wage claims and sensitive data. Evidence: input/control totals, calculation/audit log, approver/release IDs, submission receipt, bank acknowledgement, reconciliation and corrected document.
- **Benefits/pension/carrier and reimbursement:** accountable Benefits Operations specialist; collaborators benefits broker/carrier, payroll, Finance, Legal/Privacy. Systems: benefits admin, carrier EDI/API, document/evidence vault, payroll, AP/expense. Write election/eligibility/claim records and outbound carrier file; read carrier acknowledgement/bill and reconcile. Human review for life event/dependent proof, exception enrollment and claim/payment; risks are coverage lapse, discrimination, tax treatment and health-data confidentiality. Evidence: election attestation, proof decision, carrier acceptance/error response, deduction match and paid/recovered transaction.
- **Compensation/equity/recognition:** accountable Compensation specialist (Equity Administration for awards); collaborators manager, HRBP, Finance/budget owner, payroll, legal/tax, external plan administrator. Systems: compensation planning, HRIS, equity platform, recognition vendor, payroll. Write proposed effective-dated award/rate change, validate budget/range/plan, then publish only approved transaction. Manager plus delegated budget/compensation approval; heightened risk for executive pay, insider/equity controls, equal-pay policy and tax. Evidence: proposal/version, policy/budget validation, delegated approvals, vendor/plan acknowledgement and payroll posting.
- **Configuration/compliance/audit:** accountable Payroll/HRIS Controls lead; collaborators Tax, Legal, InfoSec, internal audit, local country owners and vendor. Systems: rule/configuration repository, test tenant, change management, compliance calendar, audit archive. Read policy/jurisdiction rules; write only versioned configuration under change control. Require tested change, dual approval and production release authority; never auto-interpret ambiguous legal orders. Evidence: source authority, impact assessment, test results, approvals, deployment/version ID and post-run reconciliation.

## Boundary and alias notes
- “Payroll” is a journey, not a leaf: separate employee inquiry, input, calculation, approval, payment, filing, correction and reconciliation so each can route independently.
- Time, attendance, scheduling and absence own the source record through approval; payroll owns pay calculation/release. The conversion/interface is a distinct handoff outcome, not an absence approval.
- Benefits owns eligibility, elections, proof and carrier exceptions; payroll owns employee deduction execution; Finance/AP commonly owns non-HR expenses and bank disbursement. Keep only policy-sponsored/HR-owned reimbursements in this scope.
- Leave is not generic PTO: routine absence, statutory/protected leave, accommodation/disability and occupational-health cases require separate local/legal overlays and heightened document controls.
- Pension may be called retirement, superannuation, provident fund or 401(k); equity may be LTI, RSU, option or share plan; recognition may be rewards/spot award. These are aliases only after jurisdiction/plan resolution.
- Garnishment, levy, attachment of earnings and child-support order are jurisdiction-specific aliases; authority precedence and disposable-income rules cannot be globally hard-coded.
- Exclude recruiting offers, performance ratings, general travel/AP expenses, and corporate treasury execution except where they supply an approved HR transaction or payment confirmation.

## Key facts
- GSA’s official workflow identifies discrete employee actions (create/edit/amend timesheet; create/edit/cancel absence; overtime/comp/credit requests), supervisor approval/reporting, and time-administrator creation/approval of time, schedule and absence/overtime transactions. It also specifies submission and signature deadlines, supporting atomic submission, amendment, approval and exception leaves. [GSA](https://www.gsa.gov/buy-through-us/shared-services/support-services-for-commissions-and-boards/hr-links-user-guides/time-and-leave)
- Oracle documents a concrete absence-to-payroll control boundary: complete absence entitlement, run conversion to send events to Payable Time, run Time Administration, load to paysheets, then process paysheets. Unmapped take-code/TRC rows terminate processing and create a message log requiring mapping and restart—an explicit integration-exception leaf with auditable evidence. [Oracle](https://docs.oracle.com/en/applications/peoplesoft/human-capital-management/9.2.055/peoplesoft-time-and-labor/integrating-absence-management-time-labor-payroll-north-america.html)
- HMRC requires checking timely FPS/EPS submissions, payment date, pay/deductions, statutory-pay reclamation and prior payment before correcting an unexpected PAYE bill; it distinguishes correction of FPS/EPS, HMRC payment and employee pay/deductions. These should remain distinct routable correction outcomes. [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/payroll-errors/correct-mistakes)
- SAP documentation supports separate HR-owned benefit reimbursement claim and employee pay-statement access outcomes; its time-management overview places absence, attendance, workflow/approvals, payroll and ERP integration in one operating landscape, not one undifferentiated module.

## Source quality assessment
- **Tier 1:** GSA and GOV.UK are primary government operational guidance; strongest for role boundaries, approval timing and payroll correction/compliance examples.
- **Tier 1:** Oracle and SAP official product documentation are primary evidence of implementable workflow states, interfaces, exception logs and employee-facing transactions; product-specific rather than universal legal authority.
- **Tier 2:** Workday product material is an established vendor description useful for terminology/coverage, but not compliance authority.
- **Tier 3:** None relied upon; no SEO listicle used.

## Gaps and unanswered questions
- Target countries, employing entities, union/CBA rules, pay frequencies, currencies, payroll providers and statutory authorities are unspecified; these determine rule packs, filing leaves, approval limits and evidence retention.
- Need an authoritative pension/equity plan source and carrier/benefit EDI specification before finalizing election, vesting, contribution and reconciliation state models.
- Need enterprise decisions on whether expenses, financial-wellbeing advances, recognition taxation and payment release are HR, Finance or vendor-owned; the taxonomy should preserve cross-functional handoffs either way.
- Define delegation, cutoff windows, retro limits, correction recovery policy, privacy classification/retention, and whether agents may submit versus draft high-risk legal/pay actions.
