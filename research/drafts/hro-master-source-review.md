- Core HR/privacy/case: eCFR and ICO are jurisdiction-specific; ServiceNow is product documentation. Missing authoritative private-sector/global retention schedules, GDPR/other-regime matrices, legal-hold/privilege rules, and jurisdictional case confidentiality obligations.
