# Your PAYE bill is not what you expected

Every month you have to pay HM Revenue and Customs (HMRC) what you owe as part of [running payroll](https://www.gov.uk/running-payroll/paying-hmrc) for your employees.

There are things you should check if your PAYE bill is not what you expected when you view your [online account.](https://online.hmrc.gov.uk/paye/)

This guide is also available [in Welsh (Cymraeg)](https://www.gov.uk/gwallau-cyflogres).

## What to check

Make sure you sent your [Full Payment Submission](https://www.gov.uk/running-payroll/reporting-to-hmrc) (FPS) or [Employer Payment Summary](https://www.gov.uk/running-payroll/reporting-to-hmrc-eps) (EPS) in time for the account to update.

You should also check that you:

- used the date you paid your employees on your FPS report, not the date you sent it
- reported other details in your FPS correctly, for example pay and deductions
- reported your EPS correctly, for example to reclaim any statutory pay
- previously paid HMRC the right amount - you may have paid too much or too little if your reports were incorrect

### Employees starting and leaving

Check that you correctly reported any [employees starting](https://www.gov.uk/new-employee/registering) or [employees leaving](https://www.gov.uk/employee-leaving).

You may get an incorrect bill and duplicate payroll records if you made a mistake. Both are usually corrected automatically. If your PAYE bill is still wrong by the 12th of the next tax month, you can [ask HMRC for help](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/get-help-to-correct-an-employer-paye-bill).

## Correcting errors

If you find a mistake, follow the guidance to:

- [correct an error in your FPS or EPS](https://www.gov.uk/payroll-errors/correcting-your-fps-or-eps)
- [correct a payment to HMRC](https://www.gov.uk/payroll-errors/correcting-payments-to-hmrc)

You can also correct mistakes if [you paid your employee the wrong amount or made incorrect deductions](https://www.gov.uk/payroll-errors/correcting-pay-or-deductions).

## Get help to correct your PAYE bill

If you’ve checked your payroll records and cannot find a mistake, you can [ask HMRC for help](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/get-help-to-correct-an-employer-paye-bill).
