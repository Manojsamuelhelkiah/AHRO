# Claiming Benefits by Employee

Learn how to start a benefit of type reimbursement.

## Procedure

1. Go to My Employee ProfileBenefits OverviewGo to BenefitsReimbursement.
2. An eligible employee can see a reimbursement benefit with number of days
remaining to claim, amount used, and remaining. Under this benefit, choose
Start a Claim.


![](https://help.sap.com/doc/6e6f99c62c644fc9a3111a3063dc8a62/2605/en-US/loio74b004dcdba34138af0c1bd5836438e3_LowRes.jpg)

3. In the claim screen, check the Entitlement Amount and
enter an amount, in the Total Claim Amount field, that
lesser than or equal to the entitlement amount.
4. Add attachments if necessary and choose Save to submit
the claim.
