### Intuitive self-service

- Provide personalized, easy-to-use tools to create, update, and track HR service tickets

- Access quick actions, HR policies, and guided experiences while creating tickets, ensuring fast resolutions

- Use Joule for intelligent answers, actionable guidance, and automatic case creation


### AI-driven case management

- Centralize HR service delivery with integrated tools in a unified workspace powered by core HR data from SAP SuccessFactors Employee Central

- Guide service reps through complex HR scenarios with intelligent case management

- Use AI for auto-classification, content summarization, emails, and recommendations to accelerate case resolution


### Insights to action

- Monitor service operations, identify employee issues, and use actionable insights to optimize processes

- Simplify administration using customizable features and low-code tools to manage and scale systems efficiently

- Ensure access to up-to-date and accurate HR policies, procedures, and recommendations
