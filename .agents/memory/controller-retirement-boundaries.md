name: Historical controller retirement
description: Retention of the removed controller must never restore authority or fictionalize volunteers
---

The user requested deletion of the controller, not concealment or a replacement
app. Retained runs, setup grants, consent and delivery outcomes are evidence
only. No historical agreement or membership bootstrap can authorize a new run,
retry, continuation, outreach or offline simulation of a retired case.

**Why:** Retention is necessary for privacy, holds, custody and truthful
sent/unknown outcomes, but retaining records must not retain operational
authority. A volunteer remains a real person after a feature is removed.

**How to apply:** Keep retired cases inert across provider boundaries,
continuations and automatic workers. Retirement must not use an offline
fallback that performs simulated actions. Preserve uncertainty and exact reply
correlation without advancement. Future physical cleanup needs a separate
lawful retention decision; never purge the Demo Organization or uploaded bytes
as a shortcut. Stop old workers before applying a retirement release.
