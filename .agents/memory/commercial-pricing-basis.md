---
name: Commercial pricing basis
description: Business rules that keep supplier pricing separate from the client's automation ROI.
---

Commercial quotes use full committed annual transaction volume as the shared denominator for annual, monthly, transaction, token, and overage figures. Supplier COGS excludes client fallback and non-integrated-system manual labor. Supplier HITL includes sampled residual review only. Overage recovers variable supplier COGS, while support, knowledge operations, and product recovery remain fixed recurring allocations. MCP/action consumption belongs in the channel/integration bucket, not pure model cost.

**Why:** Mixing the Year-1 adoption ramp with full committed volume understated unit recovery, while including client manual labor overstated supplier COGS. Misclassifying MCP as model cost also made client-ready category labels misleading.

**How to apply:** Classify every new cost as client benefit/labor, supplier variable COGS, supplier fixed COGS, support, or product recovery before adding it to the quote. Keep displayed category totals reconciled to the quoted COGS.
