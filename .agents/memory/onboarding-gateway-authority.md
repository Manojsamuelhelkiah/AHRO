---
name: Onboarding gateway authority
description: Human confirmation boundaries, historical rollout, and evidence invalidation for the three onboarding gateways.
---

Offer acceptance is a preboarding prerequisite, not an ordinary optional checklist item. An initially incomplete package requires the offer-request email attempt before release; existing recipient authorization still applies. Do not equate dispatch, an inbound reply, or generic approval with confirmed package completion.

**Why:** The requested process separates offer acceptance, BGV monitoring, and Day-1 readiness. Letting welcome messages or connector recovery run before acceptance defeats the first gateway even if the screen shows a hold.

**How to apply:** Enforce acceptance at effect boundaries as well as orchestration entry. Keep blocked or uncertain email outcomes actionable for HR. Do not infer historical acceptance or replay completed cases to introduce the gate.

Final Day-1 confirmation authorizes the evidence HR reviewed, not any future evidence that later becomes clear. Reopening mandatory checks or required upload safety must invalidate previous final-review authority inside the mutation's case lock.

**Why:** Retrying a previously approved final review after an unsafe-to-safe transition can otherwise finish onboarding without a fresh human decision, especially when both changes occur between watcher ticks.

**How to apply:** Preserve an audit of the old decision, require fresh HR confirmation after invalidation, and derive visible readiness from current mandatory requirements and live evidence. Optional requirements remain non-blocking.

Business-stage terminology is a presentation boundary, not a rename of operational authority or recorded evidence. Use Offer-Acceptance Package, Background Checks, and Day-1 Readiness in current product copy; separation stages retain their own business purpose.

**Why:** Approval rules and historical completion event titles participate in matching and outcome evidence. Renaming those identifiers during a copy change could silently break authorization or invalidate historical reporting.

**How to apply:** Translate only at display and new-message composition boundaries. Keep rule references, idempotency keys, matching predicates, stored source documents, already-sent payloads, and historical records unchanged.
