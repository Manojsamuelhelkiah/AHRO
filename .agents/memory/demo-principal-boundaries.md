---
name: Demo principal boundaries
description: Public demonstration identities cannot be used as real-client or specialist authority
---

Public one-click Demo identities remain pinned to Demo Organization. Real
client selection requires a named, individually authenticated person with
explicit client-granted access.

**Why:** The user confirmed named sign-in for real tenants. Shared public
identities cannot safely hold a client grant, even when the UI offers workspace
selection. Fictional demonstrations are not professional verification.

**How to apply:** Do not promote public Demo membership or introduce synthetic
specialist grants to make a demonstration visible. Read-only demo audit views
must remain principal/case scoped rather than exposing tenant-wide governance,
private recipients, legal holds or exports.
