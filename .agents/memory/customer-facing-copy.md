---
name: Customer-facing copy
description: Content rule for Cost Explorer screens and labels.
---

Customer-facing copy must explain the product, flows, assumptions, and calculations directly. Do not refer users to internal decks, slides, architecture documents, or “documented assumptions” as a substitute for an explanation.

**Why:** The product must be understandable as a standalone application; internal-reference wording makes the interface feel unfinished and gives users no useful context.

**How to apply:** Replace internal-source references with self-contained operational language or a concise product-facing baseline label. Technical source provenance may remain in code or internal artifacts, but must not be presented as UI copy.
