---
name: Standing intake conversion
description: Product scope of administrator registration as a narrow standing instruction for new email intake conversion.
---

An active client-specific Intake Conversion Agent registration is the administrator's standing instruction to convert newly received, valid, unambiguous supported email templates into held cases. Do not add a second switch, per-intake approval, browser dependency, model spending, or repeated-registration requirement.

**Why:** The product owner explicitly distinguished a real unattended intake capability from metadata-only registration and the existing manual review workflow. Conversion is not a human decision or permission to execute the resulting case.

**How to apply:** Keep this exception narrow; unrelated agent tools retain their initiation rules. Never inherit temporary Super Admin elevation into background authority or manufacture a human review. Historical backlog stays manual, and a new registration must not revive unfinished work from an older authorization. Informational attachments remain quarantined; genuinely required evidence decisions remain human and held.
