---
name: Synthetic fairness programme boundaries
description: Why fairness evaluation starts with reviewed actual recommendation scope and cannot imply certification
---

Fairness evaluation starts with fixed fictional paired scenarios and a reviewed actual recommendation type. Business and relevant verified specialist reviewers must approve the scope and irrelevant variations before acceptance criteria are written or reviewed.

**Why:** The business-user explainer describes fairness and employee appeals as roadmap work. A pair that varies a supposedly irrelevant characteristic without domain review can test the wrong assumption; equal outputs can also be equally incorrect.

**How to apply:** Preserve staged review when expanding decision types or fixtures. Record both discrepancy and deviation from human expectations. Describe limited observations, unresolved differences and exclusions, not bias-free decisions or statistical/legal certification. Do not widen agent permissions or introduce ranking/profiling. Any real protected-characteristic analysis requires separate lawful-purpose, access and retention approval.
