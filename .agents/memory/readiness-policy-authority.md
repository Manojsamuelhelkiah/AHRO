---
name: Readiness policy authority
description: Safety and rollout decisions for weighted onboarding readiness, deadlines, and versioned reviews.
---

Treat the weighted readiness view as distinct from legacy execution workstreams. Separate documentation from BGV and HRIS from payroll; do not assign two weights to one completion signal.

**Why:** Earlier execution grouping combined responsibilities that the requested five-workstream score intentionally separates. A high score does not prove that mandatory checks or final HR review passed.

**How to apply:** Require independent evidence and retain live Gateway C checks. Explicitly exclude and normalize non-applicable weights. Historical completed cases must not be automatically replay-enrolled.

An enrolled case's snapshotted policy is its only timing authority. Changes to policy, joining dates, or evidence must not silently change already-reviewed obligations.

**Why:** Running old short simulated timers beside calendar deadlines produces contradictory breaches. Reusing an approval reference after changed evidence lets an old email become new human authority.

**How to apply:** Preserve policy/deadline history, require explicit audited rebasing, version each reopened review, and reject old-cycle decisions. Acknowledging an SLA review records human attention; it must not complete the source action or advance employment state.
