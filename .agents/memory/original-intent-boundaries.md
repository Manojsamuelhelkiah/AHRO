---
name: Original case intent boundaries
description: Preserve why a case originally existed, independently of later review questions and operational preparation.
---

Onboarding remains Onboarding when an access workstream raises a question. The predefined Application jurisdiction inquiry is a standalone application-access intake example, not an onboarding child or a detected policy violation. Unknown history must remain Not recorded.

**Why:** The product owner wants the originating business request distinguished from scenario titles, processing families, current blockers and workflow states, without inventing a requester, justification, parent, approval or completed execution.

**How to apply:** Use recorded creation/intake evidence. Preserve fictional source intent when a separate current operational request is supplied during preparation; ordinary profile-read access may disclose only the fixed taxonomy label, not its source narratives.

Fixed fictional source definitions are fingerprinted by retained preparation and activation records. Additive presentation/provenance annotations must live outside those definitions.

**Why:** Changing the shape of a fixed definition, even to add an accurate label, changes its fingerprint and invalidates previously recorded activation bindings.

**How to apply:** Keep fixed source payloads unchanged when adding metadata; store annotations on the case or project them independently. Any intentional source-definition change needs explicit compatibility handling for retained records.

Keep “Current action” separate from the original intent. It describes recorded pending, in-progress or blocked work, not a renamed originating request or a claim of execution. Unknown work must not be guessed from a title.

**Why:** The product owner requested a compact current-action indicator and its use in Analytics while preserving the original request and single-line pipeline.

**How to apply:** Prefer current open human assignments, then blocked, in-progress and pending recorded workstreams. Treat the workload as a one-case-once snapshot; exclude illustrative cases from measurements. Duration and transition-rate claims require actual transition history, not case age.
