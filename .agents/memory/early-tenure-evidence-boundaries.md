---
name: Early-tenure evidence boundaries
description: Historical authority receipts, actual-start scheduling, and safe reminder ownership.
---

Historical employment and survey evidence must retain a server-authored immutable authority receipt, rather than being validated only against today's mutable grant.

**Why:** Revoking or revising a grant must stop new writes without erasing evidence that was validly authorized when recorded. Missing historical receipts remain unverified.

**How to apply:** Authorize before looking up restricted employment or survey records; freeze the exact authority revision and context at write time. Treat contradictory employment observations as unknown unless all outstanding contradictions are explicitly corrected. Survey timing uses authoritative actual employment start, not the planned joining date.

Early-tenure reminders use member-addressed in-app delivery until a verified member-to-email binding exists.

**Why:** An active member ID paired with an arbitrary browser-provided email does not prove recipient identity. Existing organization membership does not provide that binding.

**How to apply:** Validate current tenant membership, preserve intended owner identity, and never describe a reminder as delivered merely because it was enqueued. Keep actual-start schedule revisions separate from manual schedule edits and bind probation reviews to the actual-start revision.
