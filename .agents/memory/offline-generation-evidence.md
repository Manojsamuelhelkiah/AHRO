---
name: Offline generation evidence
description: Provenance rule for records retained when a fictional example becomes operational.
---

An activated example's earlier child records remain historical even if their
timestamps are later than activation. Current evidence and access require both
the current operational generation and exclusion of original and earlier
generation identifiers. Preserve historical receipts and provider outcomes,
but do not let them approve new work or be retried as current effects.

**Why:** Example histories may contain dates representing future planned or
simulated events. Clock comparisons cannot establish that a document, token,
approval, or delivery was issued for the real worker.

**How to apply:** At each new reader, worker, portal, outcome or dispatch
boundary, check immutable generation membership as well as time and live
authorization. Unrecognized record kinds fail closed for activated examples;
ordinary genuine cases remain unaffected.
