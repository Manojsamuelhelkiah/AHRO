---
name: Outcome KPI evidence
description: Measurement boundaries for onboarding business outcomes and unavailable data sources.
---

Do not substitute live checklist state for historical Day-1 readiness, offer acceptance for first-submission document completeness, access provisioning for combined IT/assets completion, or archived prior-employment exits for new-hire attrition.

**Why:** These sources describe different events or scopes. Substitution creates plausible but incorrect success rates; the requested outcomes require historical snapshots, complete request evidence, surveys, or current employment outcomes.

**How to apply:** Keep unmeasured outcomes explicitly unavailable with baseline TBD until the corresponding evidence is collected. Exclude known simulation from measured success and disclose the cohort, usable sample, and missing evidence. Compare unrounded calculations with targets; round only for display.

Email review comparisons use recorded intake receipt cohorts, not human event-date cohorts. Keep pending counts as current status rather than reconstructed historical backlog, and do not treat held-case conversion as HR completion.

**Why:** Receipt cohorts keep older retained demand comparable with recent demand even when reviews occur in later periods. Recent cohorts are incomplete and can appear faster simply because slower requests remain pending; event-date grouping measures a different throughput question.

**How to apply:** Preserve this distinction when extending email capacity reporting. Disclose partial periods, retained-only coverage, missing evidence and sample sizes; do not infer reviewer performance or complete deletion history from these aggregates.

Authored operational-quality examples remain separate from live measurements. Compare the original observed answer with the authored expectation; a later authored correction does not turn that original observation into a match.

**Why:** The Control Tower's fixed examples explain quality risks rather than evaluate a live model. Counting corrected answers as originally correct would overstate even this limited illustrative sample.

**How to apply:** Disclose the authored sample and denominator, show corrections separately, and retain fairness limitations. Do not promote these examples to production accuracy, measured human decisions, or bias-free certification.
