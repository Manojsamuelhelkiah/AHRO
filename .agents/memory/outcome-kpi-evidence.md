---
name: Outcome KPI evidence
description: Measurement boundaries for onboarding business outcomes and unavailable data sources.
---

Do not substitute live checklist state for historical Day-1 readiness, offer acceptance for first-submission document completeness, access provisioning for combined IT/assets completion, or archived prior-employment exits for new-hire attrition.

**Why:** These sources describe different events or scopes. Substitution creates plausible but incorrect success rates; the requested outcomes require historical snapshots, complete request evidence, surveys, or current employment outcomes.

**How to apply:** Keep unmeasured outcomes explicitly unavailable with baseline TBD until the corresponding evidence is collected. Exclude known simulation from measured success and disclose the cohort, usable sample, and missing evidence. Compare unrounded calculations with targets; round only for display.
