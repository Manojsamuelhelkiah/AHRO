---
name: Case pipeline density
description: Product requirement for compact, scannable case lists rather than expanding narrative rows.
---

Keep desktop case pipeline entries on a single compact line. Long case titles and scenario explanations belong in detail views or hover text, not expanding table rows. Keep the case number and offline-example distinction visible; compact stage indicators must retain their full business meaning.

**Why:** The product owner explicitly found the expanded text-heavy pipeline clunky and requested the earlier single-row presentation.

**How to apply:** When adding case metadata, preserve list density and bounded text rather than stacking new descriptions or labels. Use readable compact cards on narrow screens instead of forcing the desktop table into the page width.
