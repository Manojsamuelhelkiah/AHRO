---
name: Fictional demo lifecycle boundary
description: When Demo Center can operate without claiming a real-person sensitive lifecycle approval.
---

Only predefined Demo Center runs with no selected volunteer may bypass the real-person sensitive lifecycle policy gate. The case must remain marked as an offline Demo Controller fixture with no candidate contact authorization. Volunteer-backed runs and user-entered synthetic cases remain behind the normal approved-policy envelope.

**Why:** The shared Demo Organization cannot invent privacy or legal authority, but fixed application-owned fictional fixtures contain no real person whose retention basis needs approval. Treating both paths identically made the safe walkthrough unusable, while broadly exempting “demo” data would allow real contact or free-text data to evade governance.

**How to apply:** Keep the exemption at the Demo Controller persistence boundary and verify the durable fixture markers on every later write. Any feature that introduces a volunteer, recipient, upload, or user-entered person data must use the ordinary lifecycle gate.
