---
name: Action Center conversation boundaries
description: Independent read-only conversational phase, separate from operational exchanges and shared working context.
---

Action Center conversations are governed annotations and read-only triage, not operational instructions, evidence, approvals, or a dispatch channel. Catalogue selection is descriptive and must distinguish a role, its current registration, and actual implemented runtime tools.

**Why:** The user approved an independent conversational foundation specifically so it would not wait for Gmail offboarding exchanges, restore operational chat execution, or overlap the separate shared working-context effort.

**How to apply:** Keep business messages out of execution-context metadata and facts/handoff projections. Reuse authorized explanation adapters; do not forward opaque chat as provider-bound grounding. Route suggestions to existing case controls for fresh human review. Never infer execution or new authority from a saved message, selected principal, or a confirmation such as “yes.”

Do not reclaim ambiguous model attempts merely because a lease elapsed.

**Why:** A provider may have processed a request even when the app lost its response. A retry that obtains a new claim could spend twice and append conflicting replies.

**How to apply:** Preserve a stable submission identity, durable claim and explicit interrupted/uncertain outcome. Retries read the original result; a new intentional question is distinct from automatic replay.

A fresh history page is not authorization for older cached pages.

**Why:** Notes access may remain valid while provider disclosure is withdrawn or evidence changes. Refreshing only the newest page and merging it into older history leaves previously disclosed excerpts visible indefinitely.

**How to apply:** Revalidate every displayed page or discard the older cached pages on refresh. Denied pagination clears confidential state, and late reads from earlier refresh epochs must not restore it.
