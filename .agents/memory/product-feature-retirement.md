name: Permanent case-test feature retirement
description: Retired product capabilities must not return through new HRO case types or demo intake.
---

Fictional case creation, seeding/reset, response progression, UAT fixture execution,
fairness runs and synthetic policy benchmarks/drift are permanently retired product
capabilities. Shared Demo accounts are limited to authorized non-operational history.
Commercial what-if modeling, developer-only regression fixtures, genuine callbacks
and separately authorized operational channel checks are not retired.

**Why:** The product owner requested feature retirement, not terminology changes,
data cleanup, live-delivery substitution or new authority for demo identities.

**How to apply:** Future HRO/offboarding entry points must not reintroduce these
actions. Preserve provenance, offline and controller fences, effect/audit history,
restricted evidence access, privacy retention and real-outcome exclusion. Do not
erase compatibility identifiers or immutable historical source text to satisfy
a search. Never run a legacy reset as cleanup.

Retirement overrides email-by-default for historical test records, not for genuine
intake. Legacy names alone do not establish that a shared eligibility rule is
exclusive to the retired feature.

**Why:** Concurrent email-default work shared eligibility logic between ordinary
delivery and samples; blanket disablement blocked genuine provider-stub checks.

**How to apply:** Inspect all callers before disabling a legacy-named helper.
Reject trusted historical/offline ownership first, then preserve ordinary recipient,
privacy, governance and provider eligibility checks.
