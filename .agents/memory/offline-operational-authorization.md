---
name: Offline example operational authorization
description: Current reporting-only scope and the distinction between historical counts and operational authority.
---

Reporting inclusion and operational authority are separate product decisions.
Authorized fictional offline examples belong in case totals and applicable
cohorts without requiring activation or repairs to individual historical records.
Reporting requests do not implicitly request operationalization.

**Why:** The owner prioritizes totals that reconcile with visible cases and accepts
imperfections in individual historical examples. Live operationalization is
a substantially larger, separately authorized change.

**How to apply:** Include authorized fictional offline examples in case counts,
status breakdowns and applicable cohorts. Keep UAT and retained real/controller
isolation, current access rules and measured-evidence safeguards. Do not repair
individual historical records merely to improve reporting. If operationalization
is requested separately, preserve original history and require explicit
named-user activation without any effects; never adopt fixture approvals,
simulated results or retired consents. Genuine UAT-run ownership and retained real
volunteer/controller identities remain isolated. Shared Demo accounts cannot
perform live transactions. Creation/reset, UAT execution, fairness and benchmark
launches remain retired. Unavailable connectors remain disclosed blockers;
offboarding closure still requires the supplied and approved section 4.6.

Authorized case-status reporting must not exclude records solely because they
are offline. Applicable outcome cohorts may include them, but fictional,
simulated or missing evidence remains unknown. Only valid post-activation
verified evidence may establish measured business success.

**Why:** Case availability, source provenance, operational activation and outcome
evidence are separate product concepts, not one offline flag.
